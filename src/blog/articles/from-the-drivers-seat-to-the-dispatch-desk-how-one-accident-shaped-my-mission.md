Having spent a good stretch of my life on the road, I know trucking is more than just a career—it's a lifestyle. The long hours, changing landscapes, and responsibility of hauling freight across the country create a unique bond among drivers. Before I began helping truckers succeed at Iron Wing Dispatching, I lived that life myself, turning wheels and tracking miles like the rest.

## My Journey—From Company Driver to Owner-Operator

Before joining Iron Wing Dispatching, I started as a company driver, gaining experience behind the wheel of a big rig. I adapted to the demands of Over-The-Road (OTR) work, learning to manage strict delivery windows, unpredictable weather, and hours-of-service regulations. Eventually, I made the leap into owning my own truck. That meant maintaining a reliable vehicle, securing profitable loads, and managing my own dispatching strategy.

Those years on the road taught me what many truckers already know: Good dispatching can make or break a driver's profit margin. Finding the right loads, mapping out routes, and staying ahead of industry changes can turn a long week into a good one—or a good month into a great quarter. Handling those tasks myself as an owner-operator showed me the value of well-planned logistics support, even though I didn't yet know how that experience would shape my future.

## The Accident That Changed My Course

Life behind the wheel isn't without its risks. I eventually suffered a fractured T12 disk in my lower spine. Although the injury wasn't catastrophic, it left me unable to tolerate the long, continuous hours of sitting that come with OTR driving. Stepping away from trucking wasn't easy. I loved the freedom of the open road, the camaraderie among drivers, and my part in moving goods across the country. But I saw an opportunity: If I couldn't drive full-time, I could find another way to support the industry and the people I cared about.

## Transforming a Hardship into My Mission

My injury inspired me to put my hands-on knowledge and experience to work helping other drivers succeed. I knew firsthand the challenges owner-operators and small carriers face, and I saw how dispatching services could help. By focusing on optimizing loads, negotiating better rates, and providing around-the-clock support, I realized I could bring value to those still turning the wheels.

My understanding of a trucker's reality—the hours, stress, and financial pressures—now guides my work with the team at Iron Wing Dispatching. My goal is to support the drivers who are still out there, making it happen day in and day out.

## Building a Better Road Ahead

My story is the foundation for why I do this work at Iron Wing Dispatching. My shift from driver to dispatcher keeps one truth front and center: Every load is handled by real people with real challenges and real dreams. By sharing my story, I hope to make it clear that my work is about more than numbers on a spreadsheet. It's about helping drivers navigate their journeys successfully, every mile of the way.
