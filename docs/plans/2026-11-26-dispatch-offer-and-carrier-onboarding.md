# Iron Wing dispatch offer and carrier onboarding

Implementation plan · November 26, 2026 · US carrier market · Prices in USD.

## 1. Commercial decisions and launch scope

| Item | Decision or working assumption |
| --- | --- |
| Dispatch Essentials | Approved name for Essential. Standard fee of 5% of the rate confirmation total on loads Iron Wing books. Includes sourcing, negotiation, routing, basic paperwork and business-hours support. The carrier handles billing. |
| Dispatch + Back Office | Approved name for Full-Service. Standard fee of 10% on the same base. Includes Essentials, dedicated dispatcher, priority 24/7 support, invoicing, factoring submissions, detention/lumper claims and compliance assistance. |
| Fleet pricing | Separate inquiry for 3+ trucks. A fleet can need either service level. Preserve the underlying service selection and negotiated fee. |
| Fee base | Approved for new agreements. Apply the percentage to the total on the load’s agreed rate confirmation. Count fuel, accessorials and reimbursements when they appear in that total. Use the total once, whether the broker itemizes the charges or quotes a flat amount. |
| Black Friday | **Approved.** Charge 3% of the rate confirmation total on eligible Dispatch Essentials loads booked through December 31, 2026. The standard 5% resumes for loads booked starting January 1, 2027. |
| Scope of the discount | Proposed for new carriers on the standard Essentials agreement. A web application or a phone request for an identifiable dispatcher callback qualifies through 11:59 p.m. Central on Monday, November 30. A timely request can be verified after the deadline. No code for the carrier to remember. The proposal excludes custom fleet agreements and Back Office. |
| Load cutoff | Use the booking date in America/Chicago. Eligible bookings through December 31, 2026 receive 3%. Bookings from January 1, 2027 receive 5%. A December 30 booking delivered January 2 gets 3%. Existing fee-due, cancellation and weekly debit rules still govern collection. |
| Commercial tradeoff | On $20,000 in eligible rate confirmation totals, fees are $600 versus $1,000 at 5%. The owner is effectively spending $400 in foregone revenue on that carrier. Review first loads and January retention before judging the promotion. |

**Example used across channels.** A rate confirmation showing $2,000 linehaul and $400 fuel totals $2,400. The fee is $72 at the promotional 3%, $120 at the standard Essentials 5%, or $240 at the Back Office 10%. A flat $2,400 confirmation produces the same fees. Show the confirmation total, percentage and fee on each statement line. A carrier asking about an ambiguous confirmation gets a dispatcher review.

**Expiry.** Use exclusive boundaries of December 1 at 00:00 Central for claims and January 1 at 00:00 Central for bookings. After enrollment closes, direct new visitors to standard pricing. Keep timely claims visible to staff and eligible carriers. If the promotion cannot be ready by launch, ship the evergreen changes separately and leave the time-limited ad off.

## 3. Inquiry, claim and lead handoff

**Keep three distinct actions.** Pricing is browsing. The existing popup is for a written question: label it **Ask a dispatcher**, with **Your question**, **Email for our reply**, **Phone, if you’d prefer a call (optional)** and **Send my question**. Carry the offer and trigger location into the inquiry. An offer question only becomes a claim when the carrier asks to apply or requests the qualifying callback. Make that choice explicit. A website application or Jack callback request produces the claim receipt.

**Public signup.** Ask for name plus the chosen callback number or email. Trailer type is an optional qualifier. Collect truck count, MC and remaining details on the dispatcher call or in onboarding. Accept short and single names. Let a direct visitor choose a plan inline or select “Discuss with dispatcher.” Say “A dispatcher will contact you about your truck and lanes. You can request the offer now and send documents later.” Show receipt and submission time only after durable acceptance. Keep entered data on a retry. Preserve existing plan URLs and historical labels.

**Backend and Outreach.** Update `iwd-backend/src/formsController.js`, router, Prisma fields, validators, notifications, and conversions together. Existing signup expects last name, email, fleet size and a plan. Exercise phone-only and email-only paths all the way through delivery; avoid fabricated email addresses or names to satisfy legacy schemas. Keep required email and message on ordinary contact inquiries. Preserve Turnstile, abuse controls and existing clients. Use a retry-safe request ID.

**Proposed record contract.** Carry the stable plan key (`ESSENTIAL`, `FULL_SERVICE`, existing custom `FLEET`), displayed plan, offer/version, source channel, original receipt time, contact preference, available attribution, source reference and qualification state. Derive eligibility and rates server-side. Use the web server's accepted time or the trusted call timestamp at the qualifying request; preserve it through delayed processing. Store times in UTC and evaluate business dates in Chicago. A URL parameter, ad click or AI-generated note alone cannot authorize a discount.

Reconcile phone and web requests to the same Iron Wing carrier where identity is established; retain both source records and the earliest valid claim. Keep ambiguous matches for the closer. Surface the claim, status and promised terms on the lead, callback task and onboarding handoff. Separate a saved inquiry, qualified carrier, signed account and first booked load. An unsuccessful downstream delivery retries without losing a lead or issuing a second claim. Verify the deployed website-to-Outreach path as well as Twilio’s existing outbox. Cover legacy contacts without email or complete attribution.

## 6. Kelevo load data, interim billing and statements

**Engineering dependency.** The checked-in Kelevo export in `src/interimBilling/ops/kelevo-prod/01-iwd_billing_loads.sql` includes rate, entered linehaul, delivery and cancellation fields but no booking timestamp. The classifier uses a carrier-level fee. Inspect the deployed export and authoritative booking event before changing either. A created, delivered, invoice or payment date is insufficient to resolve the December boundary.

- Identify the accepted booking timestamp and the agreed rate confirmation total. Verify the meaning of the exported rate against itemized and flat confirmations. Extend the scoped read-only export if necessary. Use the agreed total once and retain the source confirmation reference or revision. Hold a billing line for review if its required total or promotional booking date is missing. When a revised confirmation changes a billed total, reconcile the difference under the original booking schedule.
- Resolve the signed schedule per carrier and booked load. Persist the booking timestamp, agreement/version, fee base in cents, basis points, eligibility and calculated fee with the billing line. Preserve legacy lines, corrections, cancellations and credits against original terms. Apply the existing integer-cents rounding. Never charge both the weekly interim runner and a future permanent billing system for the same load.
- Keep booking-date **rate selection** distinct from **when fees become due**. Continue the agreed weekly Friday charge and itemized statement for delivered loads, payment by card/ACH, and the existing cancellation rules. For a $2,400 rate confirmation booked December 30 and delivered January 2, the promotional fee is $72 on the applicable January billing run. A January 1 booking for the same total costs $120 on standard Essentials or $240 on Back Office. Broker cancellation → existing no-fee/credit path. Carrier cancellation after acceptance → apply the contract rule with the original booking schedule.
- Exercise preview, approval, retry and duplicate-event handling across `classify.js`, `kelevoReader.js`, `money.js`, run service, statement/email and Stripe charging. Bench-test a mixed-rate week; reconcile each statement line and total to signed terms before enabling real collections. Keep existing Stripe payment mandates and signed history intact.

**Fallback.** If a surface quotes conflicting terms, pause the promotional ad and new promotion entry points. Keep the evergreen page and ordinary intake available, preserve recorded claims and signed terms, and give the closer the list requiring follow-up. If billing cannot resolve a load, hold that line for review. Never roll back signed economic terms by flipping a marketing flag.

**Completion means** a carrier can discover the service, ask a question or apply, receive the same quote from Jack and a dispatcher, sign the correct packet, finish setup, and see the exact promised fee on a statement. The release record should demonstrate that journey from both a phone lead and a web lead.
